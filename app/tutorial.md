## 📦 **Product Management**

### **Adding New Products**

#### **Step 1: Access Product Addition**

**Location:** Dashboard → Inventory → Add Product

1. **Navigate to inventory section** from dashboard
2. **Tap "Add Product"** button
3. **Choose input method:**
   - **Barcode Scanning:** Use camera to scan product barcode
   - **Manual Entry:** Type product information manually

#### **Step 2: Product Information Entry**

##### **Method A: Barcode Scanning** 📱

1. **Grant camera permission** when prompted
2. **Position barcode** within camera frame
   - Ensure good lighting conditions
   - Hold device steady for 1-2 seconds
3. **System searches database** for existing product
4. **If product found:**
   - Select variant from available options
   - Choose existing lot or create new one
   - Add quantity to existing stock
5. **If product not found:**
   - Choose "Add Manually" to create new product
   - Fill in product details form

##### **Method B: Manual Entry** ⌨️

1. **Tap manual input option**
2. **Enter product search term** in search field
3. **If product exists:** Select from suggestions
4. **If new product:** Continue to product form

#### **Step 3: Product Form Details**

##### **Basic Information**

- **Product Name:** Clear, descriptive name
- **Barcode:** Product barcode number (if available)
- **Measuring Unit:** kg, pieces, liters, etc.
- **Product Image:** Upload product photo (optional)

##### **Variant Information**

- **Variant Name:** Size, color, or other distinguishing features
- **Variant Code:** Internal code for variant identification
- **Pack Size:** Number of units per pack (default: 1)

##### **Inventory Details**

- **Stock Quantity:** Current available quantity
- **Low Stock Alert:** Minimum quantity before alert
- **Location in Store:** Physical location reference

##### **Pricing Information**

- **Buy Price:** Cost price from supplier
- **Sell Price:** Retail price for customers
- **MRP:** Maximum Retail Price
- **VAT:** Tax percentage (if applicable)
- **Include VAT in Price:** Whether VAT is included in sell price

##### **Supplier Information**

- **Select Supplier:** Choose from existing suppliers
- **Or create new supplier** if not in database

##### **Additional Settings**

- **Low Stock SMS:** Enable SMS alerts for low stock
- **Not Sold Notification:** Alert after X days without sale
- **Manufacturing Date:** Production date (if applicable)
- **Expiry Date:** Product expiration date (if applicable)

#### **Step 4: Save Product**

1. **Review all information** for accuracy
2. **Tap "Save Product"** to add to inventory
3. **System confirmation** message appears
4. **Product appears** in inventory list

### **Managing Existing Products**

#### **Viewing Product Details**

**Location:** Dashboard → Inventory → Show Inventory

1. **Browse product list** or use search function
2. **Tap information icon** next to product name
3. **View complete product details:**
   - Stock quantity and pack size
   - Low stock alert settings
   - Last sold date
   - Notification preferences
   - All pricing information

#### **Editing Product Information**

1. **From product details view** tap "Edit" button
2. **Modify any product details:**
   - Update pricing information
   - Change stock alert levels
   - Modify notification settings
   - Update variant information
3. **Save changes** to update product record

#### **Managing Product Lots**

**Location:** Product Details → Show Lots

1. **Access lots view** from product details
2. **View all available lots** for the product:
   - Manufacturing dates
   - Expiry dates
   - Stock quantities per lot
   - Purchase prices per lot
3. **Add new lot** if receiving fresh stock
4. **Update quantities** for existing lots

#### **Stock Management**

1. **Monitor stock levels** from inventory view
2. **Receive low stock alerts** when quantities drop
3. **Update stock quantities:**
   - Add new stock when receiving shipments
   - Adjust for damaged or expired products
   - Track stock movements and history

## 🏦 **Debt Management & Credit Sales**

### **Understanding Debt in Bebsapati**

The Bebsapati app includes a comprehensive debt management system that allows businesses to handle credit sales, track customer debts, and manage payment collections efficiently. This system is crucial for businesses that offer credit terms to regular customers.

### **Adding Debt During Checkout**

#### **Step 1: Accessing Credit/Debt Option During Sale**

**Location:** During Checkout → Summary Page → Payment Options

1. **Complete your sale process** as usual by scanning/adding products to cart
2. **Proceed to checkout** by tapping "Next" button
3. **In the Summary Page** you'll see payment option buttons:
   - **Receipt Button:** Complete immediate payment (PAID status)
   - **Draft Button:** Save as unpaid transaction (UNPAID status - creates debt)

#### **Step 2: Creating Debt Transaction**

##### **Method A: Using Draft Button for Credit Sales**

1. **Select Customer:** Choose specific customer from dropdown or create new customer
   - **Walk-in Customer:** Anonymous customer (limited debt tracking)
   - **Registered Customer:** Full debt tracking and management
2. **Apply Discounts:** Add any applicable discounts before finalizing
3. **Tap "Draft" Button:** This creates an unpaid transaction
4. **System Actions:**
   - Transaction status set to "UNPAID"
   - Debt automatically added to customer's account
   - Receipt generated showing due amount
   - Inventory updated (stock reduced)

##### **Method B: Partial Payment Processing**

1. **Record Partial Payment:** Enter amount received from customer
2. **Calculate Remaining Due:** System calculates outstanding balance
3. **Create Debt Record:** Remaining amount added as debt
4. **Update Customer Account:** Debt balance updated automatically

#### **Step 3: Debt Transaction Details**

##### **Information Captured**

- **Customer Information:** Name, contact details, customer ID
- **Transaction Details:** Products purchased, quantities, prices
- **Financial Information:**
  - Total amount
  - Amount paid (if any)
  - Due amount
  - Applied discounts
- **Due Date:** Payment deadline (configurable)
- **Transaction Reference:** Unique ID for tracking

##### **System Processing**

1. **Debt Record Creation:** New debt entry in customer's account
2. **Receipt Generation:** Shows "UNPAID" status and due amount
3. **Customer Notification:** Optional SMS/email notification
4. **Inventory Update:** Stock levels adjusted immediately
5. **Analytics Update:** Debt metrics updated in dashboard
