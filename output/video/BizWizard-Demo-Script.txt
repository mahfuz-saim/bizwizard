Cash Flow projects the balance over the next ninety days using recent activity,

SCREEN ACTIONS
Open Financing. Show the request fields, then scroll to the saved Ranked options.
Point to eligibility, the explanation and the cost/affordability fields. Show

NARRATION
If the owner needs financing, the first step is eligibility and repayment
capacity. The application screens products against the shop's records and
the requested amount and term. AI can help rank eligible options and explain
trade-offs. Code calculates pricing and repayment amounts. Deterministic
lender and borrower policies can negotiate for up to three rounds. The owner
reviews the terms before applying. This is a simulated financing market:
no external lender receives the request, and acceptance creates an application
and schedule rather than a disbursement. The displayed APR is an approximate
annualized cost measure.

NARRATION
The owner can also ask why performance looks weak. This page combines product
economics with candidate explanations. A verified insight means the code
checked how much of the observed gap the explanation accounts for. It does
not establish causation. The scenario tool lets the owner explore a price
change before making that change in the business. Its result is a modeled
estimate. That distinction matters: an estimate can support a decision
without pretending that the business has already earned the money.
